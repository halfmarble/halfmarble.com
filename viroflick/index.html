Triage

Hold & fire

A relentless single-file flood in lanes. Hold a pool to auto-fire straight up its lane — but only two lanes at once, so triage which to cover and swap fingers as threats shift. Tuned to play easily with a tremor: hold, don't aim.

What's // inside

●Charge & overcharge

Hold an orb to charge a cell — it hits harder and pierces, carrying leftover punch to the next pathogen. Hold too long and it overcharges and locks. Release in the window.

●Nine pathogen kinds

Rushers, dividing cells that split on death, shielded shells, worm segments, and a satellite-ringed strain that morphs element every time you hurt it.

●Medical Textbook

A built-in reference for every cell, pathogen, case, and tactic — with live mechanism loops, so you can study the counter cycle before you're under fire.

●Game Center leaderboards

Optional ranked scores, kept per case combination. Leave Game Center off in Settings and your scores stay entirely private.

●Built for unsteady hands

Designed in large part for people living with Parkinson's: debounced buttons that forgive tremor double-taps, and the hold-to-fire Triage case — hold a lane, don't aim — made specifically for shaky hands.

●No accounts, no ads, no tracking

ViroFlick collects no data about you — no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs. Settings and scores live on your device.

●One honest unlock

Free to download with two cases — Triage and Sepsis, free to play through surge 22. Every case is unlocked free while the beta runs. At launch, one purchase lifts the cap and opens all six: $3.99 for the first two weeks, then $4.99 — tied to your Apple Account, no subscriptions, no consumables.

Private by design

There are no accounts and no cloud sync. Your settings and best scores are stored on your device only — the same local-first stance behind everything halfmarble builds.
