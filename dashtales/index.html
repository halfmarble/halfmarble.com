How // it works

●Pick your packs at home

Download story, book, and game packs over Wi-Fi before you leave. Each pack is a curated little library your companion knows inside-out.

●Talk, hands-free

Ask for a story about a dragon who's afraid of heights. Play a quick word game. Ask a simple follow-up. It talks back, not just plays audio.

●No signal? No difference.

The brain and the packs live on your phone. National parks, mountain passes, the long empty stretch — your packs stay sharp the whole way.

Honest // out loud

Offline, DashTales switches to the brain that lives on your phone. Your packs stay sharp — your books, games, and stories are always with you. What changes: no live traffic, weather, news, or scores, and general questions get simpler answers. When it isn't sure, it says so.

Kids Mode never uses the cloud. Voices in Kids Mode never leave the phone. Purchases and exit sit behind a parental gate.

DashTales is hands-free entertainment for passengers and stopped vehicles. It is not a driver-assistance, alertness, or safety product. Always follow local law and give the road your full attention.
