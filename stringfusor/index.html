What's // inside

●A ladder of patterns

From the two-color zigzag to the four-by-four diagonal — over a dozen named pattern families, each priced by how hard it truly is to build on purpose.

●The Pattern Forge

An on-device generator that invents brand-new symmetric patterns nobody has cataloged, tests that they can actually be built, and offers them as one-off targets. Endless, and entirely on your phone.

●A living sky

The reactor floats over a night-side Earth with real city lights and drifting clouds, a phase-lit Moon, and point-cloud galaxies — all rendered live, with an original synthesized score.

●Game Center, optional

Daily and all-time leaderboards if you want them. Leave Game Center off and your scores stay entirely on your device.

●No accounts, no ads, no tracking

StringFusor collects no data about you — no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs. Settings and scores live on your device.

●One honest unlock

The Progression track — the complete daily game — is free forever. One purchase, Fusion Core, opens the Experiment track and the Pattern Forge: $3.99 for the first two weeks, then $4.99 — no subscriptions, no consumables.

Private by design

There are no accounts and no cloud sync. Your settings and best scores are stored on your device only — the same local-first stance behind everything halfmarble builds.
