What we make // Software for iPhone and iPad

There are two ways to build software for hands that shake. Design the touch so a tremor cannot spoil it: hold instead of aim, no timers, undo without limit. Or take the hands out of it altogether and use your voice. We do both.

Voice // 01 // iPhone

SteadyHeartBeat

Your heart rate, read aloud. SteadyHeartBeat speaks your live heart rate from AirPods Pro 3, with no watch, no chest strap and no glancing at a screen. Everything runs on your iPhone, and your data never leaves it.

● OPEN BETA ON TESTFLIGHT
● SPOKEN ALOUD — NO SCREEN NEEDED
● 100% ON-DEVICE — DATA NEVER LEAVES
● CYCLING ZONES & BOXING ROUNDS, CALLED ALOUD
● OPEN SOURCE, APACHE-2.0
SEE STEADYHEARTBEAT
Voice // 02 // iPhone

DashTales

Offline entertainment for the car, hands-free. Books, games, stories and simple conversation, all living on the phone, so it keeps working where there is no signal at all. You talk to it: no hands involved, which is exactly the point.

● IN DEVELOPMENT — iPHONE APP
● FULLY OFFLINE — NO SIGNAL NEEDED
● BOOKS, GAMES, STORIES, SIMPLE TALK
● PACKS YOU BUY ONCE — NO SUBSCRIPTION

Touch // 03 // iPhone & iPad

ViroFlick

A casual flick-and-defend arcade game. Read the pathogen swarming your patient, arm the one cell type that counters it, and flick a homing cell to hold the line. You hold rather than aim, so a shaky finger still plays it well.

● OPEN BETA ON TESTFLIGHT
● HOLD, DON'T AIM — BUILT FOR TREMOR
● SIX CASES, RUNS FULLY OFFLINE
● iPHONE & iPAD, iOS 17+
SEE VIROFLICK
Touch // 04 // iPhone & iPad

StringFusor

A casual color-matching puzzle game set in deep space. Steer falling strings of colored pieces, stack them, and build patterns that fuse and release their energy. No timers and unlimited undo, so nothing is lost to a bad moment.

● OPEN BETA ON TESTFLIGHT
● NO TIMERS, UNLIMITED UNDO
● PLAYS AT YOUR OWN PACE
● iPHONE & iPAD, iOS 18.6+
SEE STRINGFUSOR

The one piece of hardware

SteadyHandTool

We are a software company with one thing you can hold. SteadyHandTool is a high-stability soldering aid for fine SMD work: heavy, damped, and steady enough that small parts stay exactly where you put them. Open hardware, so anyone can build one, improve it, or make it fit their own bench.

● CERN-OHL-S-2.0 (OPEN SOURCE)
● HIGH-MASS VIBRATION DAMPENING
● BUILT FOR PRECISION PCB WORK

Giving Back // 5% of Net Profits

halfmarble gives 5% of net profits to the organizations whose research our work stands on. Not charity in the abstract: reinvestment in the commons we build from.

2.5%
Public Health Collaboration (PHC)
The low-carb, real-food metabolic health community that informed halfmarble's approach to fasting, TCR, and meal timing.
2.5%
Team Fox
The grassroots fundraising program of The Michael J. Fox Foundation, the largest non-profit funder of Parkinson's research. They fund the science halfmarble learns from. Team Fox also receives 10% of the net profit from the open-source SteadyHandTool. halfmarble is an independent Team Fox third-party fundraiser.

Gerard Ziemski

Cofounder // Platform Architect

Bachelor of Science, Honors Computer Science degree from Western University in 1998, plus two years toward an M.Sc. — dropped out to join Apple. Twenty-five years writing software. New Media Lab. Apple. Sun Microsystems. Oracle. Two patents along the way. Early open source contributor to gl4java and jogl — Java bindings for OpenGL — back when Java on the desktop still felt like the future.

Diagnosed with Parkinson's Disease over 10 years ago. It is why every app here is built to work with unsteady hands, and why I know when one doesn't.

Agentic coding tools brought back the part of this job I loved most. Twenty-five years in, I am shipping more, and enjoying it more, than I have in a long time.

Born in Poland. Educated in Canada. Cut my teeth in Silicon Valley. Now building halfmarble from Charlottesville, Virginia.

Off the clock: hiking, kayaking, books and games, tinkering with microcontrollers, and love for the company of dogs.
